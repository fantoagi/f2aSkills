# Reader Value Rubric v2

Score each item 0-2. The ninth dimension is a quality signal, not permission to invent drama:

- 0 = absent or generic
- 1 = present but broad or weak
- 2 = specific and useful

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Reader specificity | Can I identify the role and situation in one sentence? |
| Problem urgency | Does the article explain why this matters now? |
| New judgment | Does it change how the reader evaluates the topic? |
| Actionability | Can the reader take a safe next step? |
| Boundary clarity | Does it say when the advice does not apply? |
| Evidence density | Are important claims supported or properly qualified? |
| Originality | What did this article add beyond the source or common summary? |
| Scan value | Do title, first paragraph, headings, and ending communicate the promise quickly? |
| Curiosity / tension | Is there a source-backed contrast, open question, surprise, or unresolved problem that earns continued reading without clickbait? |

## Thresholds

- Lite: minimum 10/18 and no dimension 0 for reader specificity or new judgment.
- Standard: minimum 13/18 and no dimension 0 in reader specificity, new judgment, actionability, or evidence density; for A-explainer, B-analysis, and C-commentary, `curiosity_tension` is also a required non-zero dimension.
- High-risk: minimum 15/18 and no dimension 0 in any dimension.

For B-news, C-faithful, and C-retelling, “H” may be an information hook or the source’s original hook; if there is no reliable additional tension, state that explicitly in the Brief and use an information-type title. A low score is repaired by changing the brief, angle, structure, or evidence—not by adding generic filler paragraphs.
